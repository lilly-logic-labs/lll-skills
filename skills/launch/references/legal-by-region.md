# Legal and privacy pages by region

A checklist of what to expect on a launched site or app, not legal advice. Laws and dates change; confirm each item against the official source or with counsel, and mark it **in force**, **prepare** (a later effective date) or **unknown**. The agent never writes legal text; it checks that pages exist, name the real entity and match what the product does.

## Every region

- [ ] Privacy policy: what is collected, why, legal basis or purpose, who receives it (processors, analytics, ads), retention, how to access, correct or delete data, contact. Matches the real SDKs and trackers on the site (check the network tab, not the policy).
- [ ] Terms of service or sales terms where the product sells or has accounts.
- [ ] Legal entity name, registered address and contact details in the footer or an imprint page.
- [ ] Cookie and tracking notice, with consent before non-essential tracking where required.
- [ ] Refund, cancellation, shipping and returns policy if the product sells anything.
- [ ] Accessibility statement where required (public sector, EU accessibility act scope).
- [ ] A way to make a rights request and a named person who handles it.
- [ ] Breach plan: who decides, who is told, within what time.
- [ ] Children: if under-age users are possible, age gate and parental-consent flow per local rules.
- [ ] Third-party licences and attributions shipped (open-source notices in apps).

## European Union and United Kingdom

- [ ] GDPR / UK GDPR: lawful basis per purpose, records of processing, data processing agreements with vendors, transfer mechanism for data leaving the EU or UK, data-protection contact (and a representative if you have no EU establishment).
- [ ] ePrivacy cookie rules: prior opt-in for non-essential cookies and similar storage, with a reject option as easy as accept, and withdrawal as easy as consent.
- [ ] Breach notification to the supervisory authority within 72 hours where required.
- [ ] Consumer rights for sellers: pre-contract information, withdrawal right for distance sales, clear total price including tax, order confirmation.
- [ ] Imprint (Germany, Austria and some others), VAT number display, and the online dispute-resolution notice where it still applies.
- [ ] EU accessibility requirements for covered consumer services.

## United States

- [ ] State privacy laws (California and others): privacy notice, notice at collection, opt-out of sale or sharing, honour global privacy control signals where required, a request channel.
- [ ] Children's privacy (COPPA): no collecting data from under-13s without verifiable parental consent.
- [ ] Email: CAN-SPAM requires a physical address, a clear unsubscribe and honest headers.
- [ ] Sales tax and marketplace rules per state if selling; accessibility lawsuits are common, so keep the accessibility checks honest.
- [ ] Health, finance or education data bring sector rules (HIPAA, GLBA, FERPA); flag to the owner, do not interpret.

## India

- [ ] IT Rules 2021: a grievance officer named on the site with contact details; acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve within 15 days for intermediaries.
- [ ] Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 for sellers: seller legal name, address and customer-care contact; refund, cancellation and shipping policies; grievance acknowledgement within 48 hours and resolution within a month.
- [ ] CERT-In Directions (2022): a point of contact, system logs kept for a rolling 180 days within India, clocks synced to NTP, reportable cyber incidents reported within 6 hours of noticing.
- [ ] Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the Rules: phased commencement, so mark each duty in force or prepare after reading the Gazette dates. Duties include itemised plain-language notice at collection, consent that is as easy to withdraw as to give, a deletion route, breach reporting to the Board and affected people, verifiable parental consent for under-18s, and retention of certain logs for a minimum period.
- [ ] Payments: the Reserve Bank bars merchants from storing card numbers; use gateway tokens.
- [ ] Prices shown to consumers include GST and invoices carry the GSTIN; confirm with the accountant.

## Other regions to ask about

Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA and provincial laws), Australia (Privacy Act), Singapore (PDPA), UAE, Saudi Arabia, South Africa (POPIA), Japan (APPI), South Korea (PIPA), China (PIPL, with data-localisation rules). If the product serves users there, ask the owner whether counsel has reviewed it. Do not guess.

## How to record

Per item: region, rule name, status (in force / prepare / unknown), page or evidence, owner. Gaps in legal text go to Owner actions, never to autonomous edits.
